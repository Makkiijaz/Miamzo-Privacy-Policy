Privacy Policy

Miamzo · Last updated October 11, 2026

Who we are

Miamzo (“we”, “us”) is a meal-planning and grocery app for households. This policy explains what the Miamzo mobile app collects and how that data is used.

What stays on your phone

Household answers, pantry items, the grocery list, chores, and meal plans are stored on your device. If you create or join a shared household, that kitchen’s plan, list, chores, member display names, and a device id are also stored on the Miamzo API so the other phones in the household can sync.

Clearing data or leaving the household on this phone removes the local link. Email support@miamzo.app if a shared household should be deleted from the server.

Information you provide

During setup you may enter household size, food preferences, a calorie goal, budget targets, a preferred store, meal times, and pantry items. You may add grocery items and mark chores complete, including an optional photo. That content stays on the device unless it is sent to a service described below.

Meal plans and pantry scans

To generate a meal plan, or to read a pantry photo, the app sends that request to the Miamzo API or, when the app is configured to do so, to Anthropic. The request can include your household preferences, pantry names, and the photo you chose to scan.

Store prices

In the United States, a price lookup can send an item name and an approximate location to Kroger so the app can show a shelf price from a nearby store. We do not sell this information.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple or Google and, when configured, by RevenueCat. We learn whether Miamzo Premium is active. We do not receive your full payment card number.

Permissions

Notifications are optional and stay on the device: a 7:30 briefing and a cook reminder at the dinner time you chose.

Location is used only if you allow it, to set your market and to look up a nearby Kroger. It is not used for continuous tracking.

The camera and photo library are used only when you scan a fridge or pantry shelf, or when you attach an optional photo to a finished chore.

Children

Miamzo is not directed at children under 13, or the equivalent minimum age in your region. Do not use the app to submit a child’s personal information.

How long we keep data

Data on the phone remains until you erase it in Settings, leave the household on that device, or delete the app. There is no separate Miamzo login in this version, so removing the app data on the device is the wipe for that phone. Anthropic, Kroger, Apple, Google, and RevenueCat keep data under their own policies for the requests they receive.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@miamzo.app.
